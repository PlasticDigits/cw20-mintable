# Primary-source register

Access date for every external source below: **2026-10-31 UTC**. These are official product documentation or standards/regulator publications, read directly during this review. Product pages can change; scope is what the cited page describes on that date, not a claim that every account has the feature. Research does not certify deployed configuration. Recommendations in the report are CL8Y policy choices unless explicitly identified otherwise.

| ID | Direct official source | Supported scope and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | [Coinbase account recovery](https://help.coinbase.com/en-gb/coinbase/managing-my-account/get-back-into-my-account/account-recovery-lost-email-2step-verification) | Lost email/2-step access: ID upload and, if required, selfie; trusted contacts if enabled. ID route can take up to 24 hours, with possible inability to send for another 24 hours. Trusted-contact route has different restrictions. This is custodial account access; the page distinguishes compromised-account unlocking. Do not apply legacy Coinbase Pro 48-hour timings. |
| C2 | [Kraken sign-in recovery](https://support.kraken.com/articles/201889308-i-can-t-sign-in-to-my-account-) | Lost sign-in 2FA: existing Master Key or support request. Password reset restricts new/updated withdrawal addresses for up to 24 hours; existing addresses are unaffected. This is not a universal all-withdrawal freeze or automatic proof of external wallet ownership. |
| C3 | [Kraken Global Settings Lock](https://support.kraken.com/articles/201396877-what-is-the-global-settings-lock-gsl-) and [removal](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360024004711-how-do-i-remove-my-global-settings-lock-gsl-) | Optional account settings protection; removal timer cannot be expedited by support. Existing Master Key can unlock immediately; it cannot be newly added/changed while locked. Removal instructions apply to Kraken Pro. A separate recovery credential is useful, but its bypass means this is not equivalent to CL8Y's minimum recovery contest. |
| C4 | [Kraken withdrawal holds](https://support.kraken.com/articles/360000389606-why-is-there-a-withdrawal-hold-on-my-account-) | Distinguishes password-change/new-address holds from purchase/deposit holds, such as certain 72-hour card/digital-wallet and seven-day ACH holds. Purchase holds cover specified amounts and generally do not prevent trading. Do not combine these into a single recovery delay. |
| C5 | [Binance 2FA reset](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/2de25ddf8c5b4f59adaebe9452f7b64b) | Available-factor verification and support/review paths; withdrawals, P2P selling, internal transfers and payment services disabled for up to 48 hours after reset. This Binance.com documentation is distinct from Binance.US. |
| C6 | [Binance support video verification](https://www.binance.com/en-IN/support/faq/detail/7857b3ec78f0401bb497d5bb7d5b2994) | Support can require a case-specific video call, the KYC document previously used, and a spoken statement for reset/withdrawal requests. Preferred-language alternative is described. This regional help page documents a support procedure, not universal mandatory video recovery or proof of wallet title. |
| B1 | [Bank of America suspicious activity](https://web.bankofamerica.com/en/security/report-suspicious-activity) | Obtain contact number independently from card/statement; contact bank promptly and use relevant lock controls. Physical debit-card locking does not cover digital/virtual cards. Bank-specific customer procedure, not general law. |
| B2 | [Monzo call status](https://monzo.com/help/monzo-fraud-category/monzo-call-status-web) | Independently check a purported bank call in the app; covers calls Monzo initiates, not customer-initiated calls. Rejects requests to transfer to a supposed safe account. This check assumes the independent app/device is trustworthy; it is not wallet recovery or a guarantee against coercion. |
| B3 | [FFIEC authentication guidance, 2021](https://www.ffiec.gov/sites/default/files/media/press-releases/2021/authentication-and-access-to-financial-institution-services-and-systems.pdf) | Risk assessment, layered security and stronger authentication for higher-risk access/actions, including privileged users and third parties. Supervisory guidance expressly does not impose new regulatory requirements or interpret compliance standards. It supports risk-based step-up, not a universal KYC mandate. |
| B4 | [CFPB Electronic Fund Transfers FAQs](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/deposit-accounts-resources/electronic-fund-transfers/electronic-fund-transfers-faqs/) | For covered U.S. consumer EFTs, notice leads to reasonable investigation under Regulation E; a police report cannot be a prerequisite to beginning that investigation. Coverage and deadlines are jurisdiction/product-specific. No inference that CL8Y transfers are covered or reimbursable. |
| B5 | [Bank of America credential recovery entry form](https://secure.bankofamerica.com/auth/retrieve/) | Public form requests existing card/account linkage and SSN/ITIN, with an alternative method offered. This illustrates recovery against prior bank records. No information was submitted or authenticated recovery attempted; later risk checks and sufficiency of these inputs are untested. Do not copy static personal identifiers into a wallet-ownership proof. |
| N1 | [NIST SP 800-63B-4 §4.2, recovery](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b/events/) | Saved/issued recovery codes, recovery contacts, repeated proofing and subscriber notification. AAL2 supports specified combinations of independent recovery methods/bound authenticators, or repeated proofing where previously proofed. An account never identity-proofed cannot use reproofing as its recovery method. These are digital subscriber-account requirements, not a blockchain ownership tribunal. |
| N2 | [NIST SP 800-63A-4, proofing requirements](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63a/ial-general/) | Evidence validation, privacy risk assessment, minimum necessary data, protected storage, retention/deletion and redress; live capture/presentation and injection defenses, trained review and documented exception handling with referees/references. Liveness reduces some fraud; it does not establish past wallet entitlement or freedom from coercion. |
| D1 | [Safe RecoveryHub](https://help.safe.global/articles/9622260218-account-recovery-with-saferecoveryhub) | Optional configured Recoverer can replace owners/change threshold via Zodiac Delay Modifier; default delay 28 days, configurable. Proposal/execution are separate; execution becomes permissionless after the delay, subject to configured transaction expiry (default infinite). Cancellation requires the Safe threshold, including after the delay before execution. Initial-release notification limitation is documented. Neither independent veto nor a 30-day minimum is a Safe core guarantee. |
| D2 | [Safe modules](https://docs.safe.global/advanced/smart-account-modules) | Owner-authorized optional modules can execute via `execTransactionFromModule`. Malicious modules can take over an account. Core multisig threshold is not a proof that a particular enabled recovery module is safe. |
| D3 | [Argent onchain guardian recovery](https://support.argent.xyz/hc/en-us/articles/360007338877-How-to-recover-my-wallet-with-guardians-onchain-complete-guide) | Ethereum Vault flow: guardian approvals, 48-hour delay, independent call and comparison of emojis tied to new account public key; default service guardian uses SMS/email. Product/network-specific; dated references to other products do not establish their current recovery behavior. |
| D4 | [Argent Ethereum approval rules](https://support.argent.xyz/hc/en-us/articles/360008828238-Guardian-approvals-needed-in-Ethereum-account) | Recovery requires ceil(N/2) guardians. Cancellation requires ceil((N+1)/2) approvals across owner and guardians. With two guardians, owner plus one guardian or both guardians must cancel. This is not unconditional owner-only cancellation or proof that guardians are independent. |
| D5 | [Argent Vault migration](https://support.argent.xyz/hc/en-us/articles/29344529584925-Argent-Vault-migration) | August 2025 product notice: existing Ethereum Vault accounts remain supported in a separate app, new accounts unavailable, active feature development stopped; migration leaves contracts unchanged. Existing deployed product behavior, not a new-account recommendation. |
| D6 | [Ready recovery security upgrade](https://help.ready.co/hc/en-us/articles/32186529793565-Recovering-your-wallet-after-the-security-upgrade) | July 2026 flow describes encrypted recovery data and Turnkey-managed KEK with email verification. Explicitly excludes Argent Ethereum Vault and Ready Wallet. Backend/key-service trust differs from Vault's guardian mechanism. |

No source above promises to determine rightful ownership from a social login, identity document, or guardian quorum alone. The report's wallet-entitlement rubric is an explicit design inference. No Ethereum mechanism is assumed to be deployed or directly portable on Terra Classic.

## Proposed adjudication policy

This policy is a CL8Y design recommendation, not a claim that NIST, a bank, or a CEX requires these exact thresholds. **Trusted CL8Y multisig governance is an explicit assumption.** Governance compromise/collusion can defeat off-chain adjudication and, where upgrade authority exists, on-chain policy. Distinct roles and independent constituent keys reduce accidental or single-role abuse; role names alone do not create independence.

### Evidence and registration

Decide four separate questions: **who is this person/account; which keys can they control now; what historic entitlement links them to these exact assets; did they consent to the disputed movement?** None substitutes for another. A historic owner may legitimately sell/transfer a wallet; a current key holder may be its attacker.

Before an incident, offer optional registration linking the source to at least one independently controlled recovery wallet, preferably two independent custodial/device domains. Both source and recovery wallets sign a canonical, domain/chain-bound, expiring, one-use registration challenge. Record effective policy version, signed linkage and chain time before a delayed change matures. Candidate/old-wallet consent, cancellation and old-delay snapshots belong to portal #1; profile assignment is not on-chain recovery authority. Same seed, same compromised host or same cloud account weakens independence even with different addresses. Never request a seed phrase/private key.

| Evidence class | Useful evidence | Decision weight / required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-incident binding | Matured independent recovery-wallet registration, authenticated earlier binding records or equivalent independently dated commitments. | Strong anchor if independently verified. A freshly created profile cannot backdate ownership. Check revocation, sale/transfer and compromise of registration channels. |
| Dual possession | Fresh recovery-wallet signature plus fresh destination acceptance; source-key signature if safely available is additional evidence. | Required for the normal enrolled route; source-key availability is not required after loss. If destination equals recovery wallet, require a separate independent corroborating channel rather than counting one key twice. Two signatures are not necessarily two independent factors. |
| Historic entitlement/consent | Independent exchange withdrawal record verified through the issuer; earlier signed agreements/commitments, counterparty records, custody history, private transaction-intent records. | Require a second independently verified source of linkage and investigate contradictory evidence. Public chain history can be copied; screenshots, shared balances and knowledge of transactions alone are weak. |
| Identity proofing, optional | Prior identity binding, issuer/document authenticity checks, live capture/in-person verification with presentation/injection defenses, or a privacy-preserving verifier attestation. | Proves the stated identity to a defined scope. Does not prove historic wallet title. New KYC cannot repair missing linkage by itself; a fresh verifier response must bind the case and prior record where applicable. |
| Accessible exception route | Trained referee, independently verified references and prior records, accessible in-person/assisted process without mandatory government ID or smartphone biometrics. | Manual review with the same entitlement burden and independent corroboration; neither lack of ID nor social popularity determines outcome. If evidence remains insufficient, deny execution with appeal rather than invent ownership. |
| Social/email/phone | Prior independent contact route, suspicious changes and communication continuity. | Notification/corroboration only. Recovered social login, SMS, police-report assertion, guardian relationship or matching name alone never authorizes recovery. Contact through independently obtained prior channels, not contact details supplied only in the claim. |

Normal approval requires an independently verified pre-incident linkage, a second independent entitlement/consent source, fresh destination control/acceptance, independent panel review and no unresolved material contradiction. Unenrolled/lost-all-keys cases go to exception review; at least two independent historical linkage sources and separate reviewers must corroborate entitlement. There is no numerical score that lets many weak social facts replace a strong linkage. Refusal to approve for insufficient evidence is preferable to an unsupported forced transfer; containment is separately reviewed for proportionality and appeal.

### Case lifecycle, roles and exact authorization

1. **Intake and containment:** open a pseudonymous case. Pause-only automation can contain under #4 and alert independently registered channels; it cannot recover, unpause or change roles. Separate triage records exact token/source/amount and evidence scope, not an unbounded fleet freeze. A source-key objection is permitted even if that key may be compromised.
2. **Private investigation:** two adjudicators with distinct custody/administrative domains review provenance, competing claimants, sale/transfer, coercion and guardian independence. Recuse financial/personal conflicts. Compare issuer records through independently obtained channels; do not rely on a claimant-provided callback. Notify pre-incident channels and affected holders through safe, accessible routes. Absence of reply is not consent.
3. **Authorization:** configured governance multisig attests a redacted decision. Bind chain, controller/token identity, source, exact integer amount, recipient, evidence commitment, case/policy version, unique nonce and earliest execution. Destination separately signs/accepts that exact proposal, using the actual chain mechanism. Reserve exact source principal atomically; aggregate reservations cannot exceed balance. No social login, portal session, minter or responder can authorize movement.
4. **Contest:** minimum **30 complete days = 2,592,000 seconds** from the exact authorized proposal. Publish case ID, immutable proposal, authoritative chain deadline and redacted rationale; independent notifications go to pre-existing channels. A destination/amount/evidence change or replacement proposal starts a fresh full period. Never shorten through pause, settings, role rotation or portal clock. UI states must distinguish pending, contested, vetoed, cancelled, executable and executed.
5. **Dispute/veto/appeal:** a source key or claimant may object but cannot release reservations, change destination, restart time or obtain a permanent veto. Deduplicate repeated assertions to the same case; only new substantive evidence needs a new reasoned review. An independent designated veto authority blocks execution for that nonce and retains containment/reservation. It does not award the objector the assets. A panel other than the original decision-makers hears appeal. Target seven days for triage and fourteen days for appeal response; justified extensions must name reviewer, scope and next review date. These are service targets, never automatic release/award deadlines.
6. **Reauthorize or release safely:** vetoed nonce remains unusable. Governance can atomically replace under current authorities with full >=30d contest and no reservation gap. Replacement cannot reduce reserved principal without independent release approval; replacing 100 with 1 must not silently unreserve 99 and bypass two-role cancellation. Any increase needs available unreserved balance. Cancellation/release requires a governance request and independent adjudication confirmation; that request binds the authority epoch and must be renewed by current governance after rotation. An epoch/key rotation invalidates stale execution authority without unreserving or clearing blacklist/freeze. Same-source-key repeated objections cannot repeatedly reset the timer. Governance liveness failures and prolonged holds remain explicit trust risks; review holds periodically and provide redress, not silent timeouts that let attackers drain.
7. **Execute and account:** separate recovery executor checks current authority/epoch, immutable tuple, exact reservation, elapsed minimum, no unresolved dispute/veto and fresh destination confirmation. As operational policy, obtain destination confirmation within 24h before execution; if control is lost, stop, replace destination/proposal and restart full delay. Chain acceptance alone may not establish continued secure custody. Reject unsafe/blacklisted destination; only explicit authorized recovery bypasses source lock/blacklist/global pause. Record exact transferred units, reservation consumption, unchanged supply and receipt. Containment release/unpause is a separate authorized action. Receipt in a custodian wallet is custody; final victim delivery requires a separate agreed destination and receipt, without bypassing required recovery authorization.

Separate functions: pause-only responder; blacklist authority; governance authorization; independent dispute/veto/appeal reviewers; recovery execution; unpause; migration/key rotation; private evidence custodian. Document actual constituent-key overlap and unavoidable trust, not merely different addresses. Remove compromised responder authority promptly; newly enabled privileges and rotations use their required delays and candidate acceptance. A recovery role must not confer mint, migrate or arbitrary-call power.

Allow at least fourteen days to lodge an appeal after delivery of a reasoned decision through established accessible channels. Execution waits for both the full on-chain contest and this appeal window, with no unresolved substantive case. If no appeal is lodged, final resolution for retention means documented case closure after those windows and the execution/cancellation outcome; otherwise it means the final independent appeal decision and recorded disposition. Later evidence can support a new review and appropriate remedy, but never an unreviewed reverse transfer.

### Adversarial decision examples

| Situation | Decision and control |
| --- | --- |
| Hacked wallet, real owner has old independent recovery key | Source signature is untrusted for consent. Verify matured linkage and independent history, then destination acceptance, panel approval, reservation and full contest. Attacker source objection cannot drain, release or continually reset. |
| Hacked social identity, wallet and recovery key intact | Social claimant cannot recover or change enrolled destination. Contact prior independent channel and request independent signatures; an authentic social login alone has zero recovery authority. |
| Legitimate sale, gift or custody transfer | Original registration/history does not defeat an authenticated subsequent disposition. Investigate assignment and counterparty records. Deny clawback when consent/entitlement transferred; if contested, retain proportionate containment pending independent resolution, not automatic award to the first registrant. |
| Coercion, including real ID and valid signatures | Liveness and signatures cannot establish free consent. Offer confidential independent interview and safe contact preferences; escalate to separate reviewers, delay/stop execution when coercion evidence remains unresolved. Do not publish sensitive allegations or send an unsafe-channel alert that exposes the person. |
| Malicious claimant with copied public history and new ID | Reject absent independent prior linkage and corroboration. Do not let police-report claims, celebrity status, screenshots or many matching public facts replace it. Rate-limit intake without blocking accessible substantive appeals. |
| Colluding/compromised guardians | Quorum is corroboration, not entitlement. Check independence, recent replacements and conflicts. Guardians cannot alone authorize, execute or remove independent veto; preserve reservation while a separate panel resolves competing evidence. |
| Authority rotation or repeated attacker resets | Stale authority cannot execute, but reservation persists. Only authorized replacement restarts >=30d; repeated source objections cannot reset or permanently veto. Cancellation releases only after independent confirmation; no transient unreserved balance. |

### Privacy and evidence retention

Store raw submissions in a separate encrypted evidence service with case-scoped access, audited reads, short-lived upload access and no public object links. Portal public endpoints, chain events, notifications, logs and repository history contain no ID images, names, contact details, biometric media, police documents or private operational plans. A bare hash of a predictable ID is not anonymous: commit to a canonical private manifest with a random secret salt of at least 256 bits; store salt privately and publish only commitment/version/redacted rationale. Reviewers need a controlled private disclosure path to verify provenance. Commitments prove later consistency, not truth.

Proposed retention: delete identity images and live media after verification, ordinarily within seven days; keep only verifier outcome, method, timestamp and case binding. Retain necessary encrypted case evidence until 90 days after final appeal resolution; record narrowly justified, periodically reviewed legal-hold exceptions. Minimize irrelevant fields on collection; give applicants a private correction/deletion request route, disclose retention in advance and audit deletion including backup expiry. These are proposed operating defaults, not assertions about current storage or universal legal rules. Where provider/legal requirements differ, document purpose, minimum fields and lawful retention before collection. Preserve enough minimal signed decision/commitment history to audit execution without retaining biometric templates.

Independent appeal reviewers must be able to examine the minimized signed verifier attestation and its validation provenance. If that is insufficient to resolve a disputed verification, use fresh independent proofing or narrowly documented preservation of the necessary original until review completes; notify the applicant of the exception and deletion date. The default seven-day media deletion must not silently make an offered appeal impossible.

Ordinary transfers do **not** require KYC. Optional higher-assurance recovery proofing has an accessible non-ID route. The portal stores no signing secrets and grants no authority merely by listing a wallet. Private retention, reviewer independence and accessible appeals need implementation/evidence; this report does not claim they exist.
